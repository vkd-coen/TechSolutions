About TechPro Solutions

A family business built on decades of engineering know-how, honest service and a genuine love of technology.

Our Story

TechPro Solutions was born from a lifelong passion for technology shared between a father and son. What started as fixing neighbours' computers at the kitchen table has grown into a trusted local business serving individuals, families and small businesses across Montreal.

We believe everyone deserves honest, transparent tech support without the corporate runaround. When you contact us, you speak directly with one of the engineers who will actually work on your device — no middlemen, no call centres, no surprises on the invoice.

Meet the Team
